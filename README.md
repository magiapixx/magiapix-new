# Magiapix — AI Website Design Prompt

## Creative Direction & Tone
Build a premium, editorial digital-agency website for **Magiapix**, a creative web development studio specializing in digital brand/business visualization for ambitious brands. The feel should be cinematic, minimal, high-contrast, and artistic — closer to a design-award portfolio than a generic SaaS site. Every section should feel deliberate and spacious, never crowded. Avoid AI-copywriting clichés (no "elevate," "seamless," "unleash," "next-gen," "transformative"); use concrete verbs that describe what actually happens (e.g., "we build," "we ship," "we design," "we rebuild"). No placeholder names — use realistic project/client names and organic, specific numbers (e.g., "8.4 years," "47.2%," "142 projects") instead of round marketing stats.

## Color Palette
- Background (primary): **#ffffff**
- Near-black ink (text, hero overlay, dark sections): **#111011**
- Dark gray (secondary text, dividers): **#3e3f40**
- Mid gray (muted labels, captions): **#73746e**
- Warm stone accent (imagery tint, subtle highlight blocks): **#c0b5a8**
- Button background: **#e5e5e5** with black text
- Hairline dividers: **#e5e5e5**, 1px

Usage: pages are overwhelmingly white/near-white (~50%+), with near-black used for hero imagery overlay, nav, and heavy type moments. Warm stone (#c0b5a8) should appear sparingly — in image treatments, small accent tags, or section backgrounds — to keep the palette from feeling cold.

## Typography
- **Headings**: Geist (or closest available geometric sans), weight 600. H1 at ~50px on desktop (scale down responsively to ~32px mobile). Use tight letter-spacing, generous line-height (1.1–1.2).
- **Body**: clean sans-serif (Inter or system sans), 16–18px, weight 400, line-height 1.6, color #3e3f40.
- **Display/editorial accent**: for the hero headline, use an italic serif treatment as a secondary voice layered near a bold sans-serif line — pairing editorial serif with clean sans headline to create contrast, similar to high-end studio sites.
- Small labels/eyebrows: 11–12px, uppercase or small-caps, letter-spaced, gray (#73746e) — use only where content is genuinely categorical (e.g., a "Services" label, not decorative).

## Header / Navigation
- Transparent nav bar overlaying the hero image, fixed/sticky on scroll (background fades to white/blur once scrolled past hero).
- Logo "magiapix" lowercase, white text over hero, ~14px sans-serif, top-left.
- Right-aligned nav links (white over hero, dark once scrolled), ~12–13px: Work, About, Journal.
- Optional globe/language icon only if multilingual — omit if not needed.
- CTA button top-right: pill-shaped, border-radius ~20px, background #e5e5e5, black text, ~12–13px, subtle hover darken.

## Hero Section
- Full-bleed cinematic photographic background (dark, moody, artistic — a surreal or atmospheric landscape/brand-visualization scene that signals craft and imagination, not stock-photo business people). Height ~60–65vh, dark overlay gradient from bottom for text legibility.
- Large headline bottom-left: bold sans-serif primary line + italic serif secondary phrase, white text, ~42–50px, max-width ~600px. Copy should name the transformation Magiapix delivers for brands (ambition → visibility), framed as a journey, not a feature dump.
- Small caption bottom-left under headline: tiny uppercase copyright/studio mark, ~10px, white, letter-spaced.
- Supporting paragraph bottom-right: ~12–13px white text, max-width ~320px, right-aligned, describing the studio's approach in plain, concrete language.
- No card, no button clutter in hero — just image, headline, and two small text blocks anchoring the corners.

## About / Positioning Section
- White background, generous padding (~80–100px vertical).
- Small eyebrow label top-left: "About" with a ">" prefix, 11px, gray.
- Centered heading block, max-width ~700px, ~24–28px: first sentence regular weight stating what Magiapix is, second sentence bold stating the scope of work (brand visualization, web builds, digital product design end-to-end).
- No card wrapper — just typography on white space.

## Stats + Proof Section (asymmetric grid, NOT equal cards)
- Two-column layout, ~40% left / 60% right, container max-width ~1140px, gap ~16px.
- Left column: vertically stacked stats separated by 1px hairline dividers (#e5e5e5) — no card backgrounds. Each stat = large bold number (~36px, #111011) + small gray label (~11px) beneath. Use organic figures (e.g., "8.4+" years, "142" projects shipped, "94.3%" satisfaction, "47.2%" average lift/conversion metric relevant to client outcomes).
- Right column: two stacked full-bleed images (portrait-oriented), border-radius ~6–8px, showing atmospheric/artistic brand-visualization stills — not literal office/team photos. Subtle shadow optional, kept minimal.

## Services / Capabilities Section (bento/zig-zag, not 3-card row)
- Explicitly avoid a 3-equal-card horizontal row. Use a 2-column zig-zag: alternate image-left/text-right, then text-left/image-right, for each core capability (e.g., brand visualization, web build, product/UX design). Each block has generous vertical padding (~80px), a short concrete description of what the team actually does, and no icon-card grid.
- Alternatively, a bento layout: one large tile (flagship capability) plus 2–3 smaller asymmetric tiles — varied sizes, not uniform cards.
- Use numbered markers (01/02/03) only if the services are presented as a genuinely sequential process (discovery → build → launch); otherwise omit numbering entirely.

## Case Studies / Work Section
- Large-format image grid, asymmetric sizing (one hero project spanning 2 columns, smaller projects beside it), border-radius ~6px on all images, hover state reveals project name and one-line result in small caps white text over a dark overlay.
- Each case study entry should state a concrete outcome (e.g., "redesigned checkout flow, 47.2% faster load") rather than vague praise.

## Social Proof / Testimonial Section
- Not a 3-card grid. Use a single large quote block (editorial serif/italic for the quote, ~24px) with client name, title, and company set below in small sans-serif caps — real-sounding names and companies, not "John Doe" or "Acme Corp."
- Optional secondary row of small client logo marks in grayscale, evenly spaced, no borders/cards.

## Process Section (only if genuinely sequential)
- If included, use a clean horizontal or vertical timeline with numbered markers (01–04), each step a short concrete action ("We audit the current brand experience," "We prototype in the browser," etc.) — avoid decorative stepper graphics.

## Final CTA Section
- Full-width, background #111011 (near-black), white bold sans-serif heading (~36–42px) stating a direct next step, one-line supporting text in gray (#73746e-toned light gray on dark), single pill CTA button (background #e5e5e5, black text, border-radius 20px) with direct active-voice label such as "Start a project" or "Book a call" — not "Submit."

## Footer
- Near-black or white background (choose contrast opposite of CTA section), simple multi-column layout: logo, nav links repeated, contact email, social icons, small copyright line matching hero's small-caps style.

## Design Details
- Borders: hairline 1px #e5e5e5 dividers used for stat separation and section breaks — not boxes around every element.
- Radius: pill buttons ~20px; images ~6–8px; no heavy rounding elsewhere.
- Shadows: minimal to none — rely on whitespace and contrast for hierarchy, not drop shadows.
- Imagery: consistent cinematic, slightly surreal/atmospheric photographic treatment throughout (moody lighting, landscape or abstract brand visuals) rather than literal corporate stock photography.
- Cards: reserve elevated/bordered card treatment only for genuinely distinct elevated content (e.g., a pricing tier or a pull-quote), not as the default wrapper for every text block.
- Spacing: section vertical padding 80–100px, container max-width ~1140px, consistent generous margins for an editorial, uncluttered rhythm.
- Motion: subtle fade/slide-in on scroll for section reveals, smooth hover transitions on images and buttons — nothing bouncy or gimmicky.
